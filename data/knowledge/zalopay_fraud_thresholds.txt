ZaloPay Fraud Loss Thresholds & Alert Triggers

MONTHLY FRAUD LOSS:
Normal range: 3–4.5B VND total
Warning: >4.5–5B VND or >+20% MoM
Alert: >6B VND or sudden spike >+40% MoM

MOM CHANGE:
Normal: ±15%
Warning: >+25% MoM
Alert: >+40% MoM or >-40% (check for detection gap)

INTERNATIONAL SHARE:
Normal: 25–40% of total
Warning: >50%
Alert: >60%

DOMESTIC DIRECT SHARE:
Normal: 50–70% of total
Warning: >80% concentrated

NAPAS MERCHANT LEVEL 1 (per merchant):
Normal: <0.5%
Warning: 0.5–0.75%
Alert: fraud_amt >500M AND rate ≥0.75%

NAPAS MERCHANT LEVEL 2 (platform-wide):
Normal: <0.75%
Warning: 0.75–1%
Alert: fraud_amt >1B AND rate ≥1%

WEEKLY FRAUD LOSS:
Spike: >+20% WoW
Critical spike: >+50% WoW — investigate same-day with ACR
Drop: <-30% WoW — verify not a detection gap

PROMOTION ABUSE — WEEKLY %ABUSE:
Normal: 1.5–2.5% (totalAbuse / totalSpending)
Warning: >2.5–3%
Alert: >3–4%
Red flag: >4% — immediate action required

PROMOTION ABUSE — MONTHLY %ABUSE:
Normal: 1.8–3.5%
Investigation threshold: >5%

COIN2DD ABUSE RATE:
Normal: 3–4% (totalAbuse / totalDiscount)
Signal: >5% — investigate
SME contributing: >7% — escalate immediately

VAMP (Visa Acquirer Monitoring Program):
TC40 fraud rate approaching threshold → escalate to acquirer
NAPAS fraud rate Level 2 ≥1% → immediate escalation
